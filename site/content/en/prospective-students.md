whatTitle: What IEM is
whatBody1: The IEM bachelor's is a programme at Aalto University's School of Science. In three years you study mathematics, programming, economics and operations management, and pick a minor from any Aalto school.
whatBody2: Graduates work as consultants, product managers, analysts and founders. About half continue to a master's in Otaniemi, the rest go on exchange or into work.
applyTitle: How to apply
applySteps:
  - n: "1"
    title: Joint application in March
    body: Apply at Studyinfo. IEM is its own study option.
  - n: "2"
    title: Certificate or entrance exam
    body: About half are admitted on the matriculation certificate, half on the DIA entrance exam in May–June.
  - n: "3"
    title: Results in July
    body: Accept your place at Studyinfo and register as attending.
  - n: "4"
    title: Orientation in August
    body: The fuksi captains get in touch over the summer. The first week is full of programme.
lifeTitle: Student life
life:
  - title: Haalarit
    body: "The overalls are the IEM student's badge: a white boilersuit and a five-colour stripe. Handed to first-years in the autumn."
  - title: Sitsit
    body: A seated dinner party with songs and speeches. The fuksi sitsit are your first, not your last.
  - title: The fuksi year
    body: First-years collect fuksi points from events all year. They pay off on May Day eve on Ullanlinnanmäki.
  - title: Sauna and sports
    body: The guild has a weekly sauna slot. Floorball, football and a ski trip in spring.
faqTitle: Frequently asked
faq:
  - q: Do I have to join the guild?
    a: No, but you should. Membership is 8 € per academic year and gives access to events, the guild room and member prices.
  - q: How much maths is there?
    a: "A lot in the first year: calculus, linear algebra and probability. From the second year the emphasis shifts to applying it."
  - q: Can I come if I can't code?
    a: Yes. First-year courses start from the basics and assume no prior experience.
  - q: What are overall patches?
    a: "Badges you get from events, excursions and other guilds, sewn onto your overalls. The unwritten rule: the more, the better."
  - q: Can exchange students join?
    a: Yes. Exchange students are welcome at every event, and the international committee runs its own programme in English.
captainsKicker: Fuksi captains 2026
captainsTitle: Ask us anything
captainsBody: The fuksi captains are second-year students whose job is to make your first year a good one. We answer messages over the summer too.

Industrial Engineering and Management combines technology and business: an
engineer's tools, commercial understanding and the ability to lead people.
This page covers how to apply and what the first year looks like.

Also check out [Prodeko's mentoring programme](/en/prospective-students/mentoring-program/),
