## Doctoral programme session, April 2024

In April 2024 Prodeko organised a doctoral programme session in which the planning officer of the doctoral programme Jaakko Autio and doctoral researcher David Wunder presented doctoral studies and answered the participants' questions.

The slides of the session can be viewed from [this link](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1QPEb78NC7EF_OT0tTuYulFnSi7SnNhbs/view?usp=sharing) and the video [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1WfQmk-QP514ItuCv_T5EuMRf8EZUR1Xd/view?usp=sharing).

If any questions arise about the topics covered at the event, you can contact Jaakko ([jaakko.autio@aalto.fi](mailto:jaakko.autio@aalto.fi)) or David ([david.wunder@aalto.fi](mailto:david.wunder@aalto.fi)).

In addition, if you are interested in applying right now, you can look at open positions for example [here](https://www.aalto.fi/en/open-positions/two-doctoral-researcher-positions-at-the-department-of-industrial-engineering-and-management).
