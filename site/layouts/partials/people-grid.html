{{/* Renders a list of {name, role, roleEn} entries. Shared by the "people" and "archive" layouts so both draw from the same markup and styling. roleEn is used on an English page where the entry has one. The historical archive has only Finnish titles: those were the offices' names at the time, and most have no English form the guild has ever used. Two shapes, because the data has two. A board or a set of officials is a list of distinct offices with one person in each, so every card carries a name and a title of its own: that is the card grid. The honours archive is the other shape — "role" there is the award year, or the TIMES team the person was on, and up to seventeen people share one value — so a card per person repeats the year seventeen times and reads as noise. Those entries are gathered under the shared value instead, one compact row per year or team, in the order the data file lists them.
